Reworking the Hillside Rowing Club's membership form
BA Graphic and Communication Design, Year 2 live brief. Calder College of Art, January 2026.

The brief. The rowing club asked me to rework its paper membership form, which new members fill in at the boathouse on their first day.

Before. The form was two sides of A4 with 41 fields. It asked for the same emergency contact twice, put the health questions in small grey type at the foot of the second side, and had no space long enough for an email address. The club secretary told me she had to chase about half of new members for something missing.

What I did. I collected 30 completed forms, with the names covered, and marked every field left blank or filled in wrongly. I interviewed the secretary and two coaches about what they used. I cut the fields nobody used, grouped the rest into four short sections, and set the form in larger type with the health questions first. I tested a printed draft with 6 new members and changed the wording of two questions they misread.

After. The new form is one side of A4 with 22 fields in four sections: about you, health, emergency contact and consent. The health questions come first, in the same size type as everything else. Each answer space is sized for what goes in it.

What changed and why. In the first intake using the new form, 3 of 28 forms came back with something missing, against 16 of 30 before. Coaches now get the health answers on the day, because they are on the front. Filling it in took new members about 4 minutes, down from about 9.

I photographed the old form and the new form side by side, and one of the marked-up forms.

Next time I would test it with members whose first language is not English.
