Charity five-a-side tournament for Raise and Give week
Run by the Northfield University Football Society. Sports hall and outdoor pitches, Sunday 8 February 2026.

What it was for. Raise and Give week asks each sports club to run one event for the year's chosen charity, a local hospice. The society had never run a tournament before.

What I did. I was the tournament organiser. I wrote the fixture list, recruited and briefed the referees, kept the scores on the day and handled the money. Two other committee members ran the food stall and first aid.

How we ran it. We opened entries three weeks before and capped them at 16 teams of up to 7 players. Teams paid £35 to enter. I drew four groups of four, with the top two from each group going through to the quarter-finals. Six referees from the society each took a pitch for a session. Group games were 10 minutes one way and knockout games were 12, so the final kicked off at 4.10pm as planned.

How it went. All 16 places were taken and 104 players took part. Entry fees came to £560 and the food stall took £218. The union gave us the hall free, so the whole £778 went to the hospice. One group game was abandoned after a player twisted an ankle; first aid dealt with it and the result stood at the score when play stopped.

The union's sports officer, Priya Raman, wrote afterwards: "Every game started on time, which never happens."

If I ran it again I would add a women's tournament alongside it and take card payments on the day.
