# Spring Concert 2026: Wyvern University Chamber Choir

Wyvern University Music Society. St Aldhelm's Church, Wyvern, Saturday 21 March 2026.

## The brief

The choir's committee wanted a public concert that paid for itself and brought in people from the town, not only students. The budget was £900.

## My part

I was the concert manager. I booked the church, set the budget, ran ticket sales and led a front-of-house team of eight on the night. I did not conduct or choose the music; that was the musical director.

## How we put it on

- January: booked St Aldhelm's and agreed the programme with the musical director.
- February: opened ticket sales online and at the students' union, and put posters in 14 shops and cafes in town.
- Early March: two full rehearsals in the church to set the staging and the lighting.
- On the day: front of house opened at 6.30pm; the concert ran from 7.30pm to 9.15pm with one interval.

## How it went

We sold 212 of 240 seats. 96 tickets went to people who were not students, against 31 the year before. Ticket income was £1,484 against costs of £871, so the concert made £613 for the society. The interval ran over by ten minutes because the bar had one till.

"The best-run student concert we have hosted." Revd Martin Shaw, vicar of St Aldhelm's.

A recording of the second half is at https://example.com/wyvern-spring-concert

## Next time

I would open a second till at the interval and sell tickets at the town market the week before.
