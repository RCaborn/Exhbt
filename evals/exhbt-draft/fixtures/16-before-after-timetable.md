# Redesign of the campus shuttle timetable page

BSc Digital Media Design, Year 3 project. Pennfield University, March 2026. Client: the university's transport office.

## How it was

The old page was a scanned timetable shown as one image. On a phone the text was too small to read without zooming, the two routes were on separate pages, and nothing said when the next bus left. The transport office got about 30 emails a week asking for bus times.

## What I did

I watched 9 students look up their next bus on the old page and timed them. I sketched three versions, built the strongest as a clickable prototype in Figma, and tested it with the same task on 9 other students. I then built the final page in HTML and CSS with the web team.

## How it is now

The new page shows the next three departures from the stop you choose, in large type, at the top. Both routes are on one page, with a switch between them. The full timetable is real text below, so it can be searched and read aloud by a screen reader. Times that have already gone today are greyed out.

## What changed

Looking up the next bus took a median of 48 seconds on the old page and 9 seconds on the new one. All 9 students finished the task on the new page; 3 of 9 gave up on the old one. Emails asking for bus times fell from about 30 a week to about 8 in the first month.
