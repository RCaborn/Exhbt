# A redesigned handover sheet for the hall's night porters

BSc Business Management, Year 2 placement project. Kelham Hall of Residence, Redlake University, November 2025.

The hall manager asked me to redesign the sheet the night porters fill in at the end of each shift, so that the day team can see at a glance what needs doing.

The redesigned sheet is one side of A4. It has three boxes at the top for anything urgent: repairs, welfare concerns and lost keys. Under them is a tick list of the eight routine checks, and a single line for anything else. Each urgent box asks for a room number and a time, so the day team does not have to ring the porter to ask.

I interviewed the three night porters and the two day receptionists, drafted the sheet, and trialled it for four weeks. I counted what happened to every urgent item written on it.

Over the four weeks 57 urgent items were written on the new sheet. 52 of them were dealt with before midday the next day. The day team rang a night porter at home twice. The porters said the sheet took about three minutes to fill in.

The hall manager has adopted the sheet for all four halls from January 2026.

If I did it again I would trial it in two halls at once, to see whether the result holds with a different team.
