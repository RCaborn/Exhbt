Poster and programme for "Songs of the Sea", the Harbour Singers' summer concert
Design work, July 2026. Six pictures attached: the A3 poster, the A5 flyer, the programme cover, two inside spreads and the poster up in the harbour office window.

The concert is on Saturday 18 July 2026 at 7.30pm in the Old Customs House, Port Selwyn. Tickets are £8, or £5 for under-18s.

The poster uses a single linocut of a fishing boat, printed in dark blue on cream paper, with the title in a condensed serif cut to follow the line of the hull. The flyer and the programme cover use the same print cropped tighter. Inside, the programme sets the words of each song in two columns with the boat repeated small at the foot of each page.

The print was cut by hand and scanned; the type was set in Affinity Publisher. 150 posters and 400 flyers were printed.
