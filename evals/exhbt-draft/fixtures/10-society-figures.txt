Riverside Swimming Society: my year as President (2025 to 2026)

Riverside University. Written for my portfolio, June 2026.

When I took over as President the society had money worries and too few people coming to sessions. I set four goals for the year and tracked each one.

Membership. We ended the year with 186 paid members, the most the society has had.

Money. I set up a sponsorship deal with a local physio clinic and ran two fundraising swims. Together they raised £2,450, which paid for pool hire for the whole spring term.

Coaching. Six members qualified as Level 1 swimming teachers on courses the society paid for, so we could run a beginners' lane every week.

Satisfaction. In our end-of-year survey, 91% of members said they were happy with the sessions, up from 64% in the survey the year before.

How I did it: I split the committee into three small teams (sessions, money, socials), each with its own weekly to-do list, and I met each team lead for fifteen minutes every Monday. I also moved the beginners' lane to the first hour so new swimmers didn't have to share with the club squad.

What I'd do differently: I took on too much of the sponsorship work myself. Next time I would hand it to the money team from the start.
