Brackenford University Climbing Society (BUCS Climbing)
Freshers' Recruitment Campaign 2025: End-of-Campaign Report
Prepared by the Publicity Officer for the society committee, November 2025

Background and aims

At the end of the 2024-25 year the society had 140 paying members, down from a high of 190 before the pandemic. The committee set the publicity officer three aims for the autumn term:
1. Grow paying membership to at least 200 by the end of October.
2. Get more first-year students and more women to join, as both groups were under-represented.
3. Spend no more than the £350 publicity budget agreed with the Students' Union.

Audience

First-year undergraduates, especially those who had never climbed. Our survey of the 2024 intake showed that most people who did not join thought climbing was "for people who already climb" and that kit was expensive. So the message was: no experience needed, shoes and harness provided.

Channels and activity

Instagram: 6 short reels filmed at the Kestrel Wall climbing centre, each showing a complete beginner on their first route, posted twice a week through September. Paid boost of £60 on the two best reels, targeted at the university's location and ages 18 to 21.
TikTok: the same reels re-cut for TikTok. No paid spend.
Freshers' fair stall: a portable bouldering wall borrowed from the centre, with a "how long can you hang?" challenge and a sign-up QR code.
Taster sessions: 3 free beginner sessions at the Kestrel Wall in the first fortnight, with the centre's instructors.
Posters: 40 A3 posters in halls and the library.

Spending

Total spent: £327 of the £350 budget. The largest items were the taster sessions (£150, a discounted rate from the centre), the Instagram boost (£60), printing (£48) and fair stall materials (£69).

Results

Paying members: 140 before the campaign, 236 by 31 October, an increase of 96 members (69%). This beat the target of 200.
First-year students made up 61% of new members, up from 38% the year before.
Women made up 47% of new members, up from 33%.
Instagram followers: 1,120 before, 1,894 after.
The 6 reels had 41,300 views in total; the most-viewed reel had 14,800.
TikTok views: 9,650 in total.
Taster sessions: 118 attendees across the 3 sessions; 71 of them joined.
Freshers' fair: 312 QR sign-ups for the mailing list.
Cost per new member: £3.41 (£327 spent for 96 new members).

How new members heard about us (sign-up form, 96 responses): Instagram 54%, freshers' fair 22%, a friend 15%, posters 6%, TikTok 3%.

Retention: 6 weeks after joining, 71% of new members had been to at least one session in the previous fortnight.

What worked and what did not

The beginner reels did most of the work. The best one, showing a nervous first-year topping out on her first route, drew more comments than anything the society had posted before. The taster sessions turned interest into sign-ups: 60% of attendees joined. TikTok produced views but almost no members, so next year the effort should go to Instagram and the sessions. The posters were the least effective use of money.

Recommendations for next year

Keep the beginner-first message and the free tasters, start posting in August so the reels are already circulating when students arrive, drop the posters, and ask the Students' Union for an extra taster session.
