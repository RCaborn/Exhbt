Would ward nurses use a live bed dashboard instead of the whiteboard?

Charge nurses tracked beds on a hand-filled whiteboard. Would they trust a live dashboard enough to replace it?

  1. Interviewed charge nurses6 interviews
  2. Wrote SQL on the bed system
  3. Built the dashboardPower BI
  4. Tested it on shift3 weeks
  5. 6 of 6 nurses kept it

What I found

  • The first version showed too much, and nobody opened it twice.
  • Cutting it to the three numbers checked at handover made it stick.
  • All six charge nurses still used it a month after the trial.

What I'd do next

Ask what people check first before designing anything.
