Freshers' Week campaign for the Law Society

The Law Society asked for a Freshers' Week campaign to reach first-years who don't study law.

What I did

  1. Read the brief and past sign-ups
  2. Sketched three poster ideas
  3. Tested two with 20 students
  4. Ran posters and storiestwo weeks

What happened

  • Sign-ups rose from 64 last year to 112.
  • 41 of the 112 were not law students.
