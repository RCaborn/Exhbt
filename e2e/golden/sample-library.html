Forecasting weekly footfall for a city-centre library

Staff rotas were set by habit. Could last year's footfall predict next month well enough to plan shifts?

  1. Pulled gate counts3 years
  2. Added term dates and weather
  3. Fitted a seasonal model
  4. Tested on held-out months

What I found

  • Weekly forecasts landed within 6% of the real count.
  • Term dates explained more than weather did.
