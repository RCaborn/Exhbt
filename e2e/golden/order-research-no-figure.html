Would a second till cut the lunch queue?

Queues ran long at lunch. Would a second till help?

Aim

To find out whether a second till cuts the lunch queue.

Method

  1. Timed the queue
  2. Added a second till
  3. Timed it again

Results

  • Waits at the till fell from 11 minutes to 4.
  • Sales at lunch held steady in both weeks.

Conclusion

A second till cut the wait by more than half.
