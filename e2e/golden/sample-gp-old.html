Cutting missed GP appointments by changing when reminders are sent

Two GP practices lost about 8% of appointments to no-shows. Did the timing of the reminder text matter more than whether one was sent?

  1. Collected appointment records14,200 rows
  2. Cleaned and matched reminders
  3. Modelled no-shows by timinglogistic regression
  4. Piloted 3-hour reminders6 weeks

What I found

  • 3-hour reminders cut missed appointments from 7.9% to 6.5%.
  • Under-30s gained most: 11.2% down to 8.4%.
  • Same-day bookings were unaffected, so the change is free to keep.
