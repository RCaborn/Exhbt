Do students who space out their revision get better grades?

Revision advice is everywhere and rarely checked. Does spaced practice show up in students' grades?

  1. Designed the survey18 questions
  2. Collected responses412 students
  3. Ran a regression in Rallowing for year and subject
  4. Spaced revision: +0.4 grade points

What I found

  • Spaced revision went with averages 0.4 grade points higher.
  • The gap held after allowing for year of study and subject.
  • Grades were self-reported, which limits what this can show.

What I'd do next

Ask permission to use real module marks instead of self-reported grades.
