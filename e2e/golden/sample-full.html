Will banks and fintechs cooperate under open banking?

Open banking makes banks share customer data. Nobody knows whether banks and fintechs will then cooperate or compete.

  1. Define bank and fintech strategies
  2. Set payoff matrix with openness parameter
  3. Derive replicator equations and rest points
  4. Classify stability of each equilibrium
  5. Test access fees and market structurecomparative statics
  6. Select outcome via risk dominance

What I found

  • Openness acts through fintech disruption payoffs, not bank payoffs.
  • Two opposing forces, complementarity and substitution, decide whether the market moves to cooperation or competition.
  • Access fees can shift outcomes, depending on segment and market structure.
  • Small fintechs gain most from cooperation when switching costs are low.
