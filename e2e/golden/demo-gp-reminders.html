Does the timing of a reminder text cut missed GP appointments?

Two GP practices lost about 8% of appointments to no-shows. Nobody had tested whether the timing of the reminder mattered.

  1. Collected appointment records14,200 rows
  2. Cleaned and matched reminderspandas
  3. Modelled no-shows by timinglogistic regression
  4. Piloted 3-hour reminders6 weeks, 2 practices
  5. Missed appointments down 18%

What I found

  • 3-hour reminders cut missed appointments from 7.9% to 6.5%.
  • Under-30s gained most: 11.2% down to 8.4%.
  • Same-day bookings were unaffected, so the change costs nothing to keep.

What I'd do next
