Can open NHS data forecast A&E waiting times a week ahead?

Trusts plan staffing a week at a time. Could published data alone say how the next week would go?

  1. Pulled open dataNHS England, 6 years
  2. Cleaned in Pythonpandas
  3. Modelled seasonalitySARIMA, statsmodels
  4. Back-tested on held-out months12 months
  5. Forecast within 2.1 points

What I found

  • The seasonal model's error was under half that of last year's figure.
  • Bank holidays broke the first model, so I added them as features.
  • Winter weeks were the hardest to call.

What I'd do next

Test a simpler baseline first, to show the model earns its complexity.
