# The design lab: what it shows, and what to choose

For Rory and Alex, 26 Nov 2026. The page you were sent, "Making the site look and feel great", sets out the direction, the three levels and six questions. This note doesn't repeat them. It walks through what the lab shows on each screen, what is different from the site today, and the few choices that belong to one screen.

## What the lab is, and where to open it

The lab is a set of real pages, built with the parts the new site would be built from, filled with a made-up person: Amara Okafor, a data science graduate, and her projects. Nothing you type is saved or sent, and nothing in it is live.

Open it at **https://exhbt-preview.rory-113.workers.dev/lab/design** (it appears there once we have put it up). It works on a laptop and on a phone, and it's worth looking at both.

The bar across the top marked "Design lab" is the lab's own. It has the four screens, the three levels and a Motion switch. Motion is on when you arrive, so you see things move as they would. Switch it off to see where everything comes to rest. On a phone the bar folds into one button.

**The levels, as the lab draws them:**

- **Restrained:** today's type sizes on the new parts. On Home the example exhbt builds itself as you scroll to it, the highlighter draws itself under the headline once, menus and panels ease in, and publishing ends on the new finish (on a pale label).
- **Confident (where the lab opens):** larger type, the work raised higher, your name in the address on Home, one page changing through the eight looks as you scroll, and the publishing finish on ink.
- **Maximal:** the largest type, Home's example page running off the edge of the screen, her page's best project opening into its exhbt, a portfolio tile opening into its exhbt on the signed-in page, and "How it works" as a short film you play by scrolling.

## Screen by screen

### Home

**What it shows.** The headline "A website of your own, built from your projects." with a real portfolio page beside it on a laptop, and under the button on a phone. Then: one exhbt putting itself together; the same page in all eight looks; a CV line beside an exhbt, and what an employer sees; how an exhbt is made, from the file to the published page; the free roadmap; the prices in one line; and the address again, on ink, with her page rising from the bottom of the band.

**What is different from today.**

- The product is in the first screen at both widths. On a phone her page is shown full size, cropped to her best project, so its title and graph can be read, not shrunk to a thumbnail.
- One way in: "Start your portfolio". Under it, for someone with nothing to show yet: "Not sure what to show yet? Build your free roadmap first". "Free roadmap" is also in the top bar.
- Type your name and the big address and the page take it. On a phone the window moves up to show your name on the page.
- "Start with the file" shows real screens: the file, the first draft tagged Draft with its one line on what to check, and the page live at its address.
- Less empty space between sections.

**At each level.**

- **Restrained:** no name field; the looks change only when you pick one.
- **Confident:** adds the name field and the looks that follow the scroll.
- **Maximal:** the page runs to the screen's edge and its best project opens into its exhbt inside the window; "How it works" becomes a film.

### The finish (publishing)

**What it shows.** The page opens on the moment itself, a first publish on a paid plan, already done. The ink label reads "It's live on your portfolio." Its three checks tick in turn, and the address is in big type with Copy link as the one green button. Beside it are the live page with the "checked by exhbt" seal, the link as it looks pasted into LinkedIn, and "Add your next exhbt". "Play it again" runs it from "Review and publish". Four more moments sit behind the tabs (on a phone, a drop-down): publishing changes, which is quiet; Free, which never says live; back on the portfolio, where the tile turns from Draft to Live; and the wait while a first draft is written.

**What is different from today.** Today a publish ends on three lines in a blank panel and four equal buttons. Here it ends on the work, the link and one next step. Free says plainly that only she can see it, and offers the upgrade once.

**At each level.**

- **Restrained:** the label is pale, with no stroke or seal.
- **Confident:** the label is on ink.
- **Maximal:** the page flies out of the Publish button, and ink fills the screen for a beat before it settles. You see this only with Motion on, after "Play it again".

### The signed-in page (App)

**What it shows.** The frame every signed-in page would share: one top bar, one content line from the logo down, and a quiet footer. It has three screens:

- **Her first run:** "This is your portfolio, Amara.", with her page as it stands and a three-step strip. Beside her page is a large empty panel, "Your best project goes here", with one green button.
- **Her portfolio with work:** her address as the page heading and her best project shown large. Each tile carries Live or Draft, one Edit and a menu, and Delete asks first.
- **Account and billing on one page:** with the plans as cards.

**What is different from today.** Today each account page sets its own width and the text's left edge jumps. Here everything starts on one line. The four pink Delete buttons are gone, and a chosen plan is marked in ink, not green.

**At each level.**

- **Restrained:** today's sizes; menus and panels ease in.
- **Confident:** larger headings and a deeper shadow on her work.
- **Maximal:** the largest headings, an ink edge on her work, a tile that opens into its exhbt, and on first run her page filling in part by part.

### The kit

**What it shows.** One page of every button, field, menu, panel and colour rule, with today's version on the left and the proposed one on the right. It is a reference for building, not a screen to judge on looks.

## Choices that belong to one screen

Each has what happens if you say nothing.

1. **Home: what the first screen leads with.** Your own website, with the free roadmap as a line under the button and in the top bar; or the free roadmap first. *Your own website, as built.*
2. **Home: where the eight looks sit.** Third, straight after the example exhbt, while the name you typed is still on the page; or fifth, as the story on your page has it. *Third.*
3. **The finish: ink.** The publishing finish on ink breaks today's rule that only Home's last band is ink. *Yes, from confident; maximal's full-screen ink beat is left out, because it adds a second of waiting.*
4. **The finish: where a first publish ends.** On the panel in the editor; on her portfolio with the tile turning Live; or both, in that order. *Both: she lands on her portfolio, the tile turns Live, then the panel opens over it.*
5. **The finish on Free: is the upgrade the one green button?** *Yes, once, with "Back to your portfolio" as the quiet second.*
6. **App: where Delete lives.** In each tile's menu, asking first; or only inside the exhbt editor. *In the tile's menu, asking first.*

## For the builders: promoting each screen

The lab lives under `app/lab/design/` and `components/lab/`, with the proposed theme in `themes/exhbt-next/`, and answers only where `EXHBT_LAB=1` (`components/lab/server.ts`). Each screen's own report (26 Nov 2026) and `components/lab/home/README.md` hold the detail; this is the order and the cost.

### 0. The theme first (all screens depend on it)

Promote `themes/exhbt-next` into `themes/exhbt/exhbtTheme.ts`, run `npm run theme`, and bring the level's token values from `components/lab/lab.css` for the chosen level. Without it the display sizes, the buttons on ink and the motion tokens all come out differently.

- Also carry into the theme: the 44px toast buttons (`lab.css`), the ink mark for a chosen card (`components/lab/ChosenMark.tsx`), the selectable card's ink edge (already in the theme), and the AppShell banner padding (`app.css`).
- **Guards:** `npm run check:theme`, the layout-parity and portfolio-parity specs (the root theme reaches visitors' pages).
- **Risk:** a change to the root theme moves visitors' pages; the golden copies catch it.

### 1. The signed-in frame (App): 22 to 29 hours

- **Replaces:** `app/(app)/layout.tsx` and `app/(marketing)/layout.tsx` (`SiteHeader`/`SiteFooter` become `AppShell`, `TopNav` and the app footer), `components/site/SiteHeader.tsx`, `HeaderLinks.tsx`, `MobileNav.tsx`, `SiteFooter.tsx`. `app/(app)/account/page.tsx` and `account/billing/` become one page.
- **Moves:** the private-preview strip moves from `app/layout.tsx` into AppShell's `banner` slot in the group layouts, until launch. The lab leaves it out of the frame.
- **Later:** the first-run strip, the empty space and the tile chrome go into `components/portfolio-editor/` once it exists.
- **Risks:**
  - AppShell's `main` makes a `<header>` inside it a second banner landmark; use divs.
  - The specs expect a "Menu" button and an "Account" link that Astryx names differently ("Open navigation", a menu button).
  - SelectableCard announces plans as checkboxes, where RadioList is the right semantics for one choice.
  - Restrained now uses the lab's crisp entrances, not none.
- **Guards:** landing, phase1, phase2-editor-media, phase2-polish-2, phase2-saved-ideas, portfolio-landing, portfolio-switch (header). The /exhbts specs (phase2-customiser, phase2-editor, phase2-teammates, phase2-draft-tools, phase2-graphs, editor-inplace, data-safety, safari.webkit). The account specs (phase2-account, phase2-billing, phase2-billing-polish, phase2-referrals, password-login, admin). Portfolio-parity and layout-parity.

### 2. The finish: about 16 hours at confident, 2 more for maximal

- **Replaces:** the `.xe-live` result and the `!env.canGoLive` branch in `components/exhbt-editor/sheets/PublishSheet.tsx` and their styles in `sheets.css`, `components/editor/PublishWhere.tsx`, and `DraftingView` in `app/(app)/exhbts/new/DraftForm.tsx`. Export `DRAFT_STEPS` there and drop the lab's copy. `LinkPreview` uses the real `<base>/<slug>/preview.png`; the lab's `app/lab/design/finish/preview.png` route goes.
- **Lab-only:** opening on the finished moment (`FinishStage.tsx`'s effect for `state === "first"`) and "Play it again" stay in the lab. The product opens the moment on Publish.
- **Risks:**
  - `e2e/editor.ts`'s `publishSheet` selects `dialog[data-sheet="publish"]` and `publish()` waits for the live line inside it. About 24 specs go through that helper. Changes now end on a toast outside it.
  - Astryx's Dialog didn't take its name from `DialogHeader` after the content swap, so the lab passes `aria-label`.
  - It uses `:has()`.
  - A long address wraps to five lines on a phone.
- **Guards:** a new finish spec. It should check the address and the preview's source, exactly one primary button that copies and says so, no "live" anywhere on Free, an empty `getAnimations()` under reduced motion, axe, and focus returning to "Review and publish".

### 3. Home: 12 to 16 hours at confident (restrained alone 6 to 8; the film about 4 more)

- **Replaces:** `app/(marketing)/page.tsx` and `components/home/` (`LookPreview`, `HowItWorks`, `SkillsGapExample`). `home.css` becomes the page's own motion stylesheet, and `address.ts` moves beside `lib/hosts.ts`.
- **Risks:**
  - The page now holds three `[data-portfolio-home]` (the hero's, the looks' and the close's). The `home-look-*` targets in `e2e/portfolio-parity.spec.ts` need their root scoped to the looks scene, and their click moved to the radio (Astryx radios have no wrapping `label`). The golden copies don't change.
  - On a phone the hero window is a crop that `HeroPage.tsx` scrolls to her best project's title, by selector (`[data-section="featured"] h2`). If `PortfolioHome`'s markup changes, the crop falls back to the top of the page.
  - The assembling graph targets `BeforeAfterChart` by `nth-child`; if its markup changes, the animation stops silently and the finished state still shows.
  - The radar's Now / After buttons are made 44px from the page's stylesheet. Move that into `SkillRadar` on promotion.
  - Maximal's hero window is a nested scroller, and it bleeds past the content line (`overflow-x: clip` on the section).
  - Scroll timelines, sticky and view transitions have been checked in Chromium only.
- **Guards:** e2e home, landing, phase1 and responsive (they press the hero's button by name), seo, a11y, the parity `home-look` targets, and a new home-motion spec. That spec should check that reduced motion leaves no animations and that a typed name gives `portfolioHost`'s address.

### Order and total

The theme, then the frame, then the finish, then Home: the order on the page for Rory. At confident that is roughly 50 to 61 hours of building and tests, not counting the theme job and the portfolio editor, which other work is building.

## Where the lab differs from the page for Rory

So the page can be corrected before it is sent:

1. **Home's story order.** The page has seven steps with "An address that's yours" fifth. The lab puts the name and address into the first screen, moves the eight looks to third, and ends on the address on ink. It has no questions (FAQ) section; the prices link to the pricing page.
2. **Question 2's default, "one moving picture a page".** At confident, Home has two pictures led by the scroll: the exhbt building itself and the eight looks. At maximal it has three, with the film added.
3. **Restrained's "the radar draws itself once when a roadmap opens".** It isn't in the lab: there is no roadmap result screen, and Home's radar is drawn still.
4. **The finish on ink.** The page doesn't mention ink. In the lab the finish's label is on ink from confident (pale at restrained), and maximal adds a full-screen ink beat. This breaks DESIGN.md's "one ink section, on Home only", which the page doesn't raise.
5. **Maximal does more than the page lists.** The page lists a tile and a film. The lab adds the largest type, Home's example page to the screen's edge, an ink edge on frames, the finish's flight and ink beat, and the first-run page filling in part by part.
6. **"It's live" as one sequence.** The page describes publish, then the tile turning, then the link. The lab shows them as two separate moments (the panel, and back on the portfolio) and asks which comes first (choice 4 above).
7. **Home's main button.** The lab says "Start your portfolio", which is question 5's answer for after the new portfolio is on for everyone. Until then the page's own default is "Build your exhbt".
8. **A loading button's spinner turns until the work is done.** It appears in the kit, as Astryx draws it. The page says nothing loops; with Motion off it is still.
