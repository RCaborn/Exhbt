# Portfolio first: what changes, and when

For Rory and Alex, 26 Nov 2026. This is the plan for what you asked on 24 and 25 Nov: start people on their portfolio, edit it on the page like the new exhbt editor, and add exhbts from inside it.

## What a student sees

1. They create an account and land on their own portfolio, not the account page or the roadmaps. Logging in later lands there too.
2. It's their page as an employer would see it, in the standard look, with grey hints where the words go. A thin strip at the top says "1 Your page, about a minute · 2 Your first exhbt", and the cursor is already in their name. If they came through a roadmap with the same email, their name is filled in for them.
3. They type their name and a one-line headline straight onto the page, add a photo if they want one, and pick a look from the bar along the top. The page changes look as they choose, so there's no separate preview.
4. With no exhbt yet, the big "best piece of work" panel is drawn full size, saying "Your best project goes here", with an "Add an exhbt" button in the look's own style. That's the vision you wanted: they can see what they're building before they've built it.
5. "Add an exhbt" opens the upload, now called "Add an exhbt to your portfolio", with "Or start from a blank page" underneath.
6. They write it up in the new exhbt editor. Its back link says "Your portfolio" and makes sure the last change is saved before it goes.
7. Publishing works like this. On Free, the exhbt sits on their portfolio as a draft only they can see, with the upgrade. On a paid plan, they claim their address from the button at the top of the portfolio, then publish, and land back on the portfolio with that tile now marked Live and its own link ready to copy.

Everything on the page is edited where it sits: the intro, where they're based, About me, their education and work lines, the quote, the sections (drag them, add one, remove one) and the exhbt tiles (drag, star one into the big panel, hide one). Drafts show as dashed tiles only the owner sees. "See as a visitor" shows exactly what an employer gets, and when an employer would currently get "page not found", it says so and why. Undo goes back up to 30 steps and says what it's undoing.

The answer to Alex's question goes on every screen: you publish exhbts one at a time, and each goes on your portfolio with its own link. You never publish the portfolio itself; it saves as you type and is online once you have an address and one live exhbt.

## What else changes

The top bar has "Your portfolio" and "Roadmaps". The separate exhbts list goes, and old links to it land on the portfolio. Job links become a menu on the portfolio's own bar. On a roadmap, each project idea's button says "Add as an exhbt". Team projects move into the portfolio's menu, with a notice only when someone has asked to join. On a phone every section and tile gets one "More" button with its actions, so nothing needs a fiddly drag.

## What stays the same

Every public portfolio page looks exactly as it does now. Before we touch anything we take a copy of how each look draws a page, and every change is checked against it. The looks, the new exhbt editor, roadmaps, prices, people's addresses and their exhbts' links all stay as they are, and nobody's saved work changes shape. Nothing new is stored, so going back loses nothing.

## The order it goes live

One small fix can go out now, separately. On the live site, the "See your portfolio" link after publishing probably leads to a page that doesn't exist. It's a one-line change.

Everything else is built after the new exhbt editor is on for everyone, which is ready to go this week:

- **Days 1 and 2 (Mon 30 Nov, Tue 1 Dec):** groundwork you won't notice. The copy of every look, the on-off setting, and the page made ready to be edited.
- **Days 3 to 5 (Wed 2 to Fri 4 Dec):** the new portfolio page and the new structure are built and put live, switched off. Only you two can turn them on, for yourselves, from the current portfolio page.
- **Day 6 (Mon 7 Dec):** you both try it on a laptop and an iPhone with a short checklist, and we fix what you find.
- **Day 7 (Tue 8 Dec):** it goes on for everyone, the editor and the new structure together. One setting takes it all back in a minute, with no rebuild.
- **About a week later (around 15 Dec):** the old step-by-step builder and the old exhbts list are removed.

How sure: sure of the order, and fairly sure of the days. If anything slips it'll be the page editor itself, by a day or two. That's about a day more than the 3 to 5 days plus tests you were given, mostly the safety checks at the start. If it runs over, these go first, in this order: landing a roadmap idea's first exhbt on the portfolio (it would open in the exhbt editor, as now), the warning when the page is open in two tabs, extra phone polish, and Undo going back to a single step.

## Questions, and what happens if you say nothing

1. **Edits to a live portfolio go live as they save**, as now, and the bar says "Saved · live". A "publish changes" button would need a new kind of saved copy and a second meaning of "publish".
2. **When employers would get "page not found"**, "See as a visitor" shows how the page will look from what's published, with a line saying why it isn't online yet. The other choice is to show the drafts as they'd look once live.
3. **Education and work lines** are typed on the page; links go in a small panel from "Get in touch".
4. **Team projects** live in the portfolio's menu, with a notice only when someone asks to join.
5. **A first exhbt made from a roadmap idea** lands on the portfolio with the draft in the big panel. After the first, ideas open straight in the exhbt editor.
6. **A Free user presses "Claim your address"**: they're told it comes with a paid plan and shown the plans, and after paying there's a "Back to your portfolio" button. Sending them straight back with the name filled in can come later, with Alex.
7. **Draft tiles** sit after the live exhbts, just before "Add an exhbt", and on paid plans they can't be dragged or starred. On Free everything can be arranged, as now.
8. **The LinkedIn from the roadmap form** is already copied to the profile and shown once the page is live. We keep that, with the "Show LinkedIn" switch right next to it.
9. **The home page's "Build your exhbt" button** keeps its words. Signed out, it leads to log in or create an account, then the portfolio. Alex's call.
10. **It goes on for everyone** on the day of your laptop and iPhone check, once the checks pass, and never in the same week as the move to app.exhbt.co.uk.

The free trial that holds a username for a while isn't decided, so it isn't in this plan.
