# Exhbt product context

**What:** A web app that gives graduates a free skills-gap roadmap, then turns their own project work into a portfolio of exhbts (short, checked write-ups with a key-result chart) that hiring managers can scan in 30 seconds. Domain: exhbt.co.uk.

**Who:**
- Graduates and final-year students (UK first). They use it on laptops, often on a phone for checking results.
- Hiring managers. They never sign up; they open a portfolio or per-employer link from an application, usually on a laptop, sometimes a phone, between other tasks.
- University careers services. They buy institutional licences and want privacy, accessibility and usage data.

**Promise:** Proof, not claims. AI only structures what the user supplied; it never invents results.

**Tone:** Plain British English. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No hype words.

**Plans (6 Nov 2026):** Free (roadmaps, 3 refreshes a month, no exhbts) · Premium (up to 10 hosted exhbts + a custom link per employer; price TBC, waitlist) · Institutional (universities, £6,000–18,000/year; students get Premium) · Employers always free.

**Constraints:** UK GDPR, WCAG 2.1 AA, public pages fast on mobile, year-one fixed costs £300–500.

**Platform:** web (responsive). No native app.
